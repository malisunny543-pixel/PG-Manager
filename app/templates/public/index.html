Platform Capabilities

Built for Modern PG Operations

Comprehensive tools engineered to eliminate double allocations, streamline monthly collections, and deliver a transparent resident experience.

🏢

Properties & Branches

Centralize multiple PG locations with dedicated manager assignments, branch-level property isolation, and configurable branch utility and deposit defaults.

  • Multi-branch property portfolio
  • Branch-level manager assignments
  • Custom utility rates & deposit defaults
🛏️

Rooms & Beds

Granular inventory for single, double, and triple occupancy. Strict state machine enforces cleaning and maintenance before any vacated bed is re-allocated.

  • Atomic row-locking check-ins
  • Enforced bed lifecycle state machine
  • Warden maintenance sign-off & transfers
💳

Rent & Payments

Automated monthly invoicing with precise Decimal math. Track partial and full payments across UPI and bank transfers with live balance due calculation.

  • Automated monthly rent invoice runs
  • Partial payment tracking with balance due
  • Utility meter consumption calculations
👥

Tenant Management

Resident verification with emergency contacts and KYC proof. Transparent checkout inspections, itemized deductions, and maintenance ticket lifecycle.

  • Resident profile & roommate directory
  • Maintenance ticket tracking & alerts
  • Checkout inspection & deposit settlement

Role-Based Access Control

Double-Allocation Prevention

Targeted Notice Board

Broadcast branch-specific maintenance announcements or global hostel notices directly to resident dashboards.

Executive Reports & Audit Logs

Live occupancy rates, collection efficiency analytics, defaulters list, and immutable administrative audit trails.
